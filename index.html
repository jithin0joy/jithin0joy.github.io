Graph Partitioning & GNN Systems

MCA · Semester 3Graph theorySpectral partitioningGNN literature

Collaborative research at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham exploring Kernighan–Lin and spectral partitioning, with a proposed graph-based freelancer–job recommendation architecture. We demonstrated spectral partitioning on sample data and surveyed GNN architectures. The GNN recommender remained conceptual.

What I learned: graph representations, Laplacians and eigenvectors, partition quality, neighborhood aggregation, recommendation architectures, literature review and the importance of dataset availability and reproducible evaluation.

Collaborators: Muhammed Nadeem Mohammed Basheer (co-researcher); Jisha R C (academic guide).

The repository includes a separate 2026 reproducible educational reconstruction of graph partitioning experiments, not the original dissertation code.
